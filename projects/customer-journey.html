PORTFOLIO FRAMEWORK

Customer Journey Mapping

A practical customer journey framework that can be used to diagnose customer experience and identify automation opportunities.

Awareness

The customer first encounters the business through relevant content, referrals, advertising, search or outreach.

Consideration

The customer evaluates the offer, asks questions and compares alternatives. CRM capture and timely follow-up can reduce drop-off.

Purchase

The customer commits. Clear communication, scheduling, payment and onboarding workflows can make the transition smoother.

Retention

Post-purchase support, feedback collection, customer service and targeted follow-up help build loyalty.

Advocacy

Satisfied customers can become repeat buyers, referrers and advocates. CRM lifecycle data helps identify these opportunities.

Automation opportunities

Lead capture, qualification, reminders, appointment scheduling, onboarding, support routing, feedback collection and follow-up can be connected into a measurable workflow.
