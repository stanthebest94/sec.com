CASE STUDY 04

Power BI Operations Dashboard

Created operational dashboards tracking team KPIs, project completion rates and SLA performance.

Challenge

Leadership needed a clearer view of operational performance for recurring reviews.

Approach

Structured operational data and surfaced key metrics through Power BI dashboards for weekly leadership reviews.

Outcome

The dashboards enabled data-driven leadership reviews and made KPI, completion and SLA performance easier to monitor.

Skills demonstrated

Power BI, KPI tracking, operational reporting, data interpretation and stakeholder communication.
